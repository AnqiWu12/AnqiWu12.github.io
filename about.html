About

I'm Anqi Wu, a game composer, sound designer and audio implementer from China, based in Boston.

I trained as a classical pianist and sing as my primary instrument. At Berklee College of Music I study Game and Interactive Media Scoring and Contemporary Writing and Production, with a minor in Creative Coding. I write music, design sound, and build the systems that make them respond to the player, in Wwise, Unreal Engine and Unity. I'm growing toward audio programming.
